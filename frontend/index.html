What is a CBOM (Cryptographic Bill of Materials)?

A CBOM is a structured inventory detailing every cryptographic asset, key length, algorithm, certificate, and library in your software stack, enabling compliance with NIST PQC guidelines.

What is Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL)?

Adversaries are actively storing encrypted enterprise traffic today to decrypt it once cryptanalytically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs) become online.
